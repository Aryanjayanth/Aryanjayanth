### What I build

- **Business software.** Practice management and billing for chartered accountant firms, customer CRMs for shops, rental management.
- **Marketplaces and consumer apps.** A parcel marketplace that uses the spare luggage space on intercity buses, and an order site for a real handmade resin business.
- **Tools and experiments.** An Android app for the Nothing Phone's Glyph LEDs, an algo trading handbook with risk calculators, and 3D on the web with Three.js.

I handle the whole thing myself: product idea, UI, frontend, database, auth, deployment and fixing what breaks after launch.
